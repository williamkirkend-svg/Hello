# Farm Lasso: the sound pitch

Pitch for giving the whole game a voice (Oct 7 2026). Scope, from Will's list: music, ambience, the lasso loop
(charge, throw, rating, luck reveal, hook, struggle, catch, escape), bag, selling, buying, equipping, quests, menus,
footsteps, animal calls, and every one of the 12 celebration shows. Style: playful, punchy, satisfying. Repeated
sounds stay short, music sits under effects, ambience stays subtle, loops are seamless, and Music / SFX / Ambience
have their own volume controls.

Built the same way as the aura upgrade: one shared kit, one authored asset pack, cues wired into the shows, an
install guide and a changelog. Nothing is uploaded from the cloud session; the pack is generated here, Will uploads it
once and pastes ids into one module.

## 1. What a good game soundtrack does that we do not yet

Farm Lasso is silent. Five habits from games that feel good in the hand (Peggle, Stardew, Pet Simulator, Adopt Me, the
Mario coin):

1. **Every input answers.** Hover, click, tap, zone crossing: a short bright sound under 150 ms, pitched a little
   differently each time so it never machine-guns.
2. **Rising pitch means progress.** Charge, luck ticks, quest progress and coin cascades all climb; a reward sequence
   is a scale going up, and the payoff lands a fifth above where it started.
3. **Three layers per hit.** A sub thump (felt), a transient (heard) and an air tail (shimmer, sparkle, ring-out).
   The same grammar as the aura's hot core, coloured middle and dark rim.
4. **Escalation is the reward.** Copper to rainbow, Common to Secret: the same cue grows an extra layer, a longer
   tail and a higher final note per step. The player learns the ladder by ear.
5. **The bed stays out of the way.** Music is quieter than effects and ducks for the big moments; ambience is felt
   when it stops, not while it plays; nothing loops with an audible seam.

## 2. The kit (built once, used everywhere)

`ReplicatedStorage.FarmLasso.SoundKit` (new ModuleScript). Everything plays through it.

| Capability | What it does |
|---|---|
| Groups | SoundGroups under SoundService: `Master` > `Music`, `SFX` (> `UI`, `Celebration`), `Ambience`. `SoundKit.SetVolume("Music", .5)` and `GetVolume`; the client saves them (see GameSounds). |
| Cues | `SoundKit.Play("CatchChime", {At = part, Pitch = 1.1, Volume = .8, Rate = 1})`. A cue (SoundCues) names its files, base volume, pitch spread, group, 3D rolloff and a cooldown. Variants pick round-robin, never the same twice in a row. |
| 2D and 3D | No `At` plays 2D (under SoundService). `At = part or attachment or Vector3` plays 3D from there with the cue's rolloff; a Vector3 gets a temporary anchor part. |
| Loops | `SoundKit.Loop("LassoCharge")` returns a handle: `:Set(intensity)` (maps to the cue's pitch and volume curves), `:Stop(fade)`. Loops fade in and out, never cut. |
| Pool and budget | Sound instances are pooled per cue; at most 24 one-shots (14 on touch) and 8 loops at once, oldest and quietest dropped first. The same cue cannot start twice within its cooldown (default 40 ms). |
| Ducking | `SoundKit.Duck("Music", .35, 3.5)` dips a group for a while and eases back. Celebrations Set 2 and 3 duck Music and Ambience; selling and fanfares duck Music briefly. |
| Silent fallback | `SoundIds` maps cue files to `rbxassetid://` strings. An empty id plays nothing and warns once per cue, so the game runs with a half-uploaded pack. |
| Rate | `Rate` scales PlaybackSpeed on top of pitch (the celebrations' time scale uses it). |

`CelebrationFX.AuraSound` (new ModuleScript next to AuraKit) is the show-side wrapper: `S.Cue(ctx, t, "Name", o)`
schedules a cue on the show's timeline at `ctx.Base` (or `o.At`), scales volume by `ctx.Quality`, drops the air
layer on far shows, follows `ctx.TimeRate` (AuraKit.TimeScale now publishes it) and stops everything with the show.
`S.Loop(ctx, t0, t1, "Name", o)` runs a loop between two beats; `S.Follow(ctx, "Name", part)` rides a seeker.

`StarterPlayerScripts.GameSounds` (new LocalScript) owns everything that needs no patch to the live scripts: music,
ambience and the fountain, random animal calls, footsteps by floor material, every button's hover and click, panel
open / close and tab switches, herd join (the `Herd` attribute), selling (`Sold` / `HerdSold`), quest flashes and the
volume settings panel. The lasso loop hooks (charge, zones, throw, reveal, tug, catch, bag) are one-line `SoundKit`
calls inside FarmLassoClient; the install guide lists each spot.

## 3. The pack

Generated by `audio/build_sfx_pack.py` (NumPy synthesis, `audio/synth.py` helpers, recipes in
`audio/recipes_game.py` and `audio/recipes_celebration.py`), written as 44.1 kHz mono OGG (music and ambience
stereo) into `audio/pack/`, with `audio/contact_sheet.png` (a spectrogram strip per sound) to eyeball the set.
Everything is deterministic (fixed seeds) so a re-run makes the same files.

Rules for the recipes: one-shots start within 5 ms (no silent heads), end at silence (no clicks), peak at -1 dBFS
with the loudness set per cue in SoundCues, not in the file. Loops are made seamless by crossfading the last 10
percent into the head. Playful means: bright transients, a little pitch bend on every pop, major-pentatonic
melodies, no harsh noise above 8 kHz.

## 4. Mixing rules (keep it quiet)

- Group defaults: Master 1, Music .45, SFX 1, UI .8, Celebration 1, Ambience .35. The settings panel exposes Music,
  SFX (UI and Celebration follow it) and Ambience.
- Music ducks to .3 for the length of a Set 2 or 3 celebration, to .5 during a reveal fanfare and the coin shower.
  Ambience ducks to .4 during Set 3. Event Horizon's SuckDrone ducks Music and Ambience to 0 and brings them back
  with the supernova.
- Other players' celebrations play at `ctx.Quality` volume (.7 within 120 studs, .4 beyond), without the bed, the
  charge-up, the title sting or the shimmer tails, and never duck anything. Only one remote show keeps its loops.
- Set 1 (the accent) gets at most three cues and no bed. Set 2 gets the detonation, the signature and the landing.
  Set 3 gets the charge-up, the bed, every beat.
- Footsteps are .25 volume, 3D with a 25-stud rolloff, and other players' footsteps are half that. Ambience animal
  calls play every 8 to 20 s from a random animal within 60 studs at .3.
- Hover sounds only fire when the pointer enters a button that is visible and active, never on touch devices.
- Every repeated cue has pitch spread (default .95 to 1.05) and a cooldown; TugTick's cooldown is 60 ms so fast tapping
  still reads as individual taps.

## 5. The 12 celebrations by ear

Beats follow the visual grammar: charge-up (inhale), detonate (the three-layer hit), new silhouette (a bed or loop
starts), signature (the thing that comes out gets a moving 3D sound), world touch (one world sound), body (whoosh,
land) and settle (tail, title sting). Times are the visual beats in each show file.

1. **Starfall Halo**: detonate with StarChime; the pillar is CelBed at .4; each falling star is a StarTwinkle that
   follows its seeker; the halo ring toss is CelWhooshS then HaloLand on the landing; stars riding the herd give one
   AnimalGeneric chirp.
2. **Thunder Stampede**: ThunderCrack on detonation, ArcCrackle while the ground arcs race, BullCharge per lightning
   bull (follows it), the chain lightning to props is CelImpact at the prop, the cage burst is CelDetonate at .6.
3. **Golden Tornado**: WindFunnel rises with the funnel (intensity by height), CoinSpiral when the coins climb,
   CoinRain while they fall, TornadoSlam on the X-pose slam, CelLand on the superhero landing.
4. **Galaxy Lasso**: RopeWhirl while the rope of stars whirls overhead (pitch follows the whirl), CelWhooshL on the
   throw, CosmicHum under the lift, ConstellationPull when the constellation is drawn out of the disc, StarChime on
   the catch.
5. **Blossom Burst**: TreeGrow as the tree grows, PetalShimmer during the petal rain, LotusBloom when the lotus
   opens, CelDetonate when the body explodes into the blossom, CelShimmer on the crowns.
6. **Event Horizon**: SuckDrone from t = 0, falling in pitch, Music and Ambience to zero; silence for the last .3 s
   before the supernova (every loop stops); Supernova with CelImpact; CelLand in the crater as the world comes back.
7. **Spirit Stampede**: PortalOpen, GhostHooves looping while the spiral runs (intensity by ghost count), SpiritBellow
   on the giant bison leap, CelWhooshL when the player rides the lead ghost, AnimalCow at pitch .6 when the real herd
   rears.
8. **Prism Supernova**: CelDetonate in three shells (three CelShockwaves .12 s apart), LighthouseSweep per coloured ray,
   GlassShatter on the shards, GemCrown at the crown, CelImpact on the freeze, ClockTick for the stutter (two ticks).
9. **Stormbreaker**: StormRoll under the cloud (intensity by cloud size), IceCrack for the ice-spike ring, ThunderPunch
   on the punch into the ground with CelShockwave twice, CelLand.
10. **Phoenix Rebirth**: CharCrackle during the char, CelDetonate at the explosion with FireWhoosh, WingFlap on every
    downstroke (follows the body), CelBed under the flight, SunDive at the apex, EmberSparkle on the golden burst,
    CelLand on the kneel.
11. **Astral Ascension**: StepChime per light step, ascending the scale, CelWhooshS per wing unfold, StarMapResolve
    when the map is drawn, CelShimmer as the herd is tied in, FeatherLand.
12. **Chrono Rift**: ClockTick looping from the sigil, ReverseWhoosh when the numerals reverse, GlassBreak on the
    shatter into the rift, ClockTick at .3 rate during the slow-down, Rewind during the plaza rewind, CelLand on the
    drop.

## 6. Hook points in the live scripts

FarmLassoClient (one line each, inside the existing handlers; the install guide shows the exact spots):
charge start (`Loop("LassoCharge")`), each frame while charging (`:Set(charge)`), zone crossing (`MeterGood` etc.),
release (`StingX`, `Throw`, `ThrowBig` at x2+), rope landing (`RopeLand` at the animal), reveal ticks (`LuckTick`
with rising pitch), reveal impact (`LuckImpactN` by `s.Tier`), fanfare (`FanfareN`), hook (`Hooked`), fight start
(`Loop("TugLoop")`), tap (`TugTick`), draining (`TugDanger`), catch (`CatchPop`, `CatchChime`, `RareRevealN` by
rarity), escape (`Escape`), bag full (`BagFull`), sell (`SellShower` then `SellDing`), shop buy / equip / need
(`Buy`, `Equip`, `NoCoins`), quests (`QuestAccept`, `QuestTick`, `QuestComplete`).

Everything else is GameSounds and needs no patch.

## 7. Open questions

- Music: a procedural loop is the placeholder; a licensed track from the Creator Store would replace one id.
- Species: all 24 roster animals have a call; a species added later plays its last word's call or AnimalGeneric
  until a recipe is added.
- Saving the volume settings needs one field in FarmLassoData (`SoundVolumes`) and one RemoteEvent; until then the
  settings hold for the session.
