# Changelog: the sound pack (Oct 7 2026)

## What changed (player-visible)

The game has sound. Every part of the loop answers: the lasso charge is a rope swish that rises with the meter, the
zones tick as you cross them, the release has its own good / perfect / mega stinger over the throw whoosh, the luck
reveal climbs in pitch and lands with a copper / silver / gold / rainbow impact and an escalating fanfare, the hook
snaps the rope and the animal reacts in its own voice, the tug-of-war is a rope-tension loop with a tick per tap and a
danger cue when the meter drains, the catch pops and chimes and then reveals the rarity (Common to Secret, each step
longer and brighter), escapes slip and fail, the bag beeps when full, selling is a coin shower and a register ding,
buying chimes, equipping clicks, a grey Need pill buzzes, quests tick and fanfare, every button hovers and clicks,
panels whoosh open and closed, tabs click. Footsteps change with the ground (grass, dirt, stone, wood) for every
character. All 24 roster animals have a call (chick to ostrich) that plays when they join the herd, when hooked, and
now and then from animals near you. Under it all: a countryside music loop (banjo, bass, shaker), birds, light wind
and the fountain, all quieter than the effects, and three sliders (Music, Effects, Ambience) behind a note button.

All 12 celebrations are scored on their visual beats: the Set 3 inhale ends in the three-layer detonation (sub, crack,
shimmer), each show has its signature sounds (the crystal halo, thunder and bulls, the funnel and coin rain, the
whirled rope, the growing tree, the black hole's drone into real silence then the supernova, the ghost hooves, the
lighthouse sweeps and gem crown, the storm and the punch, the wing beats and the sun dive, the ascending step
chimes, the clock ticks and the rewind), one world sound, the landing and a title sting. Music and ambience dip for
Sets 2 and 3 and come back.

## Rules and numbers

- Groups: `Master > Music, SFX (> UI, Celebration), Ambience`; defaults Music .45, SFX 1, UI .8, Celebration 1,
  Ambience .35; the sliders multiply those. Ducks: Set 2 / 3 Music .3 and Ambience .6; a fanfare or sale Music .5
  for 3 s; Event Horizon Music and Ambience 0 until the supernova.
- Budget: 24 one-shots (14 on touch), 8 loops; the oldest goes first. Every cue has a 40 ms cooldown (TugTick 60 ms,
  TugDanger .5 s, BagFull .8 s); pitch spread .95 to 1.05 by default; variants never repeat back to back.
- Other players' shows: volume x `ctx.Quality` (.7 / .4), no bed, no charge-up, no title, no shimmer tails, no
  ducking; one remote show at a time keeps loops.
- Set 1 at most three cues, no bed; Set 2 the detonation, the signature, the landing; Set 3 everything.
- Footsteps every 3.6 studs, volume .25 (others half), 3D 4 to 25 studs; landings louder and lower. Ambient calls
  every 8 to 20 s from an animal within 60 studs at .3 to .45.
- Time scale: `AuraKit.TimeScale` now sets `ctx.TimeRate` (its Scale, then 1); AuraSound applies it to every live
  show sound as playback speed.
- Loops in the pack are seamless (the last 10 percent crossfaded into the head); one-shots start within 5 ms, peak
  at -1 dBFS, loudness set per cue in SoundCues, never in the file.

## Scripts

New ModuleScripts under `ReplicatedStorage.FarmLasso`: `SoundKit` (groups, volumes, ducking, pooled Sounds, variants,
2D / 3D, loops with intensity curves, the budget, silent fallback), `SoundCues` (every cue by name), `SoundIds` (file
stem to asset id, generated by `audio/build_sfx_pack.py --lua`, keeps pasted ids).
New ModuleScript `CelebrationFX.AuraSound` (the show-side wrapper: Cue, Now, Loop, Hit, Bed, Duck, ChargeUp).
New LocalScript `StarterPlayerScripts.GameSounds` (music, ambience, fountain, animal calls, herd joins, footsteps,
menus, selling, quest flashes, the settings panel).
Patched: `CelebrationFX.AuraKit` (`K.TimeScale` publishes `ctx.TimeRate`); the 12 `CelebrationFX.Shows.<Id>` (sound
cues on their beats; no visual change).
Not patched (hooks listed in docs/sound-install.md section 4): `FarmLassoClient` (charge, zones, throw, reveal, tug,
catch, bag), `LassoClient3D` / `QuestLassoClient` (refusals and quest steps), `FarmLassoData` (saving the sliders).

Assets: `audio/pack/*.ogg` (PACKCOUNT files, 44.1 kHz, mono except music and ambience), `audio/pack/manifest.json`,
`audio/contact_sheet.png`; generated by `audio/build_sfx_pack.py` from `audio/synth.py` (the helpers),
`audio/recipes_game.py` and `audio/recipes_celebration.py` (deterministic).

Docs: `docs/sound-pitch.md` (the design and the cue table), `docs/sound-kit-api.md`, `docs/sound-install.md`.

Backup location: `ServerStorage.SoundBackup_20261007` (`CelebrationFX_old`; see docs/sound-install.md).

## How to test

See docs/sound-install.md section 5: Output clean of `[SoundKit]`, `[AuraSound]`, `[GameSounds]` errors; three
floor materials; the shop's pills; a forced x4 catch (`LassoTestMult = 4`) end to end; LOADOUT > TRY for each
celebration; a second player's show nearby; the sliders.
