Knockout City shut down June 2023: 12M+ players but poor retention, a small team could not make the systemic changes needed while shipping seasons, free-to-play did not produce the hoped buzz. Players said it was front-loaded with fun, repetitive after a few hours, matchmaking put solos against premades, and catching became too easy at high skill so 1v1s turned into catch stalemates until someone third-partied. The fun was the rally mind-game (catch versus fake versus curve or lob) and team setups. The unfun was the catch stalemate, team-versus-solo matchmaking, and no session-to-session progression hooks.

## (c) Real-world rule variants and their comeback mechanics

| Variant | Rules | Comeback |
|---|---|---|
| Traditional | Hit = out, catch = thrower out | Catch returns the teammate out longest, first-out first-in (WDBF) |
| Doctor / Medic | Each team has a secret doctor; hit players sit where hit | Doctor touches them to revive; if the doctor is hit, no more revives |
| Prisonball / Jailball / Bench Ball | Hit players go to a prison behind the enemy end line and can throw from there | A ball caught in prison frees the catcher (or everyone); a prisoner who hits an enemy gets out |
| King's Court / Protect the President | A leader per team, bodyguards block | None: leader hit ends the round |
| Scatter Ball | No teams, 3 steps max with a ball, hit = sit where hit | Sitting players throw balls that come to them; a hit from the floor puts you back in |
| Bombardment / Pin Guard | Bowling pins at the back, knock all enemy pins to win | Second win condition for a team that is down on numbers |
| Trench | Hit players go to a strip behind the enemy | Hit an enemy from the trench to return |
| Army dodgeball | FFA; hit on a limb = lose that limb | Head hit restores a limb |
| Gaga | Octagon pit, hand strikes, hit below the knee = out | Usually none |
| Backboard revival | Hit the enemy backboard | Longest-out teammate returns |
| Team Fireball / Revenge | Track who got whom out | If your eliminator is hit, everyone they eliminated returns |
| Outfield (Japanese, Mario Sports Mix, NES) | Out players stand on the boundary behind the enemy and can be passed to and throw | Out still participates |

## (d) Official rules summary

- WDBF cloth 2024 to 2026: 18 x 9 m, 1 m free space, 5 cloth balls of 17.78 cm, 6 on court plus 3 retrievers, 40-minute match of two halves, as many sets as possible, set ends when one side is fully out. Catch = thrower out plus one teammate returns in elimination order. Opening rush: 6 balls on the centre line, race for the 3 on your right. 10-second max hold. Blocking with a held ball is legal but losing control = out and the blocked ball stays live.
- British Dodgeball: 17 x 8 m, 0.75 m neutral zone each side of centre, 5 balls, 3-minute sets.
- NDL (US rubber): 60 x 30 ft, 4 ft neutral zone, attack line 10 ft from centre, 6 balls (4 blockers 8.5 in, 2 stingers 5 in).
- Gym default: half basketball court, 6 foam balls on the centre line, 3 to 5 minute rounds.

| Dodgeball Strikers (Aug 2025) | 27k visits, 1 concurrent | | Tiny |
| Blade Ball | 1B visits in 62 days | One ball, parry timing, 40+ abilities | Proves ball plus timing plus abilities has huge demand; classic throw-and-catch has no equivalent hit |

Takeaway: existing dodgeball games are either straight classic rules with no progression, simulator skins, or abilities with no readable ball-level variety. Nobody has shipped the Knockout City rally loop plus special balls with Roblox-native progression. Dodgeball Legends' 43M visits against 258 peak concurrent is the clearest signal that the theme pulls clicks but retention is unsolved.

## (f) Original cursed-ball ideas from the research pass

1. Debt Ball: the hit eliminates the target but the thrower is auto-eliminated in 8 s unless they catch any ball first.
2. Echo Ball: 2 s after landing it re-launches along the same path in reverse, toward where the thrower stood.
3. Gravity Well Ball: in flight it pulls every loose ball along behind it; it does no damage itself but the trailing balls do.
4. Mirror Ball: target and thrower swap positions for 10 s.
5. Whisper Ball: invisible and silent to everyone except the thrower and the locked-on target, half speed.
6. Ledger Ball: every catch adds a stack; when it finally hits, it eliminates that many players nearest the impact, both teams.
7. Chalk Ball: paints a 4-stud circle where it lands; anyone standing on chalk cannot catch for the rest of the round; the court shrinks.
8. Oath Ball: on pickup you declare one enemy; it only eliminates that player, homes on them mildly, and ignores catches from anyone else.
9. Relay Ball: cannot be thrown at an enemy until passed to two teammates within 5 s; the third throw is a guaranteed KO with a loud tell.
10. Rewind Ball: on hit the target is teleported to where they were 5 s ago and drops their ball.

Bonus: a Doctor role where catches with a specific medic ball revive from the jail bench.
